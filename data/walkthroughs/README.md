# Saved walkthroughs

Each folder here holds one building walkthrough: photos named `room__photo.jpg` plus a `walkthrough.json` with the client, visits and reviewed area sizes.

The real client photos (10 sites, Client A to J) are kept out of this public repo for privacy. Screenshots of those live runs are in `report.html`, and the anonymized run files are in `report/live_runs/`. To try the app without them, click **Load sample building** or upload your own photos.
