Walkthrough photos in. A priced, manager-approved cleaning proposal out, in minutes.

The problem

  • Every new janitorial account starts with a walkthrough and a price
  • Today: photos by email → Excel "Bid Cal" → Word proposal → owner approval
  • Half a day or more per bid; client waits 3 to 5 days
  • 45 bid emails and 90 spreadsheets since March

Hidden cost, found by reading all 90 spreadsheets:

  • Months priced as 4 weeks → labor under-counted 7.7%
  • Workers' comp applied at 0% in every bid
  • Profit swings 6% to 100% on similar jobs

What is new

  • Calculators (CleanGuru, BidLoom, Janitorial Manager): you type every sq ft. They don't read the walkthrough.
  • Photo-to-quote (QuoteIQ, CleanQuote): market averages, ~5 photos. Not our costs, no approval gate.
  • ChatGPT: guesses, does math in the model, saves nothing.

Bid Builder: up to 60 real photos · our wage, payroll taxes, workers' comp, supplies, equipment, markup · code does the math · checked against our real past bids · manager approves · injection-hardened

How the agents work

5 gpt-6-luna agents (blue) · code (gray) does every number · a manager (amber) approves

Demo: a real plant walkthrough (24 photos)

Live, Nov 23: Site Survey reads 24 photos → Takeoff builds 12 areas → code prices $3,542/mo → Revision: "drop the gazebo and aisle, 6 nights at $14" → $4,381/mo → STOP flag above $0.50/sq ft → back to a manager

Demo: what-if pricing

Move pay, supplies, equipment, insurance, taxes, overhead or profit, and the monthly fee updates instantly. No AI, no cost.

Safety: caught in live runs

Injection in notes, in a photo sign and in a revision request: quarantined, nothing changed · STOP flags need a written reason · tool range checks · token caps per agent, bid and day · 34 tests pass

Impact: the numbers

  • Half a day → minutes; 3 to 5 days → same day
  • 13 real CFM bids: app within a median +11%; surgical clinic $2,126 vs $2,102
  • Fixes 4-week months + missing workers' comp: ≈ $1,300/yr per account
  • ~20 estimator hours/month back for selling (estimate)
